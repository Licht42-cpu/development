## What this means for your interface

1. **One read.** Call `get_load_plan` for a vehicle and date. It returns
   `state` (`current` | `confirmed` | `closed` | `none`), `can_build`,
   `plan` (null exactly when `state` is `none`), `needs_review_count`,
   `unassigned_job_count` and `day`. `state: "none"` is an answer, not an
   error and not an empty plan, and a closed day is readable. Do not build
   a DTO of your own from `ns_load_plan_view`; that drift is what blocked
   you.
2. **Say which day you are showing.** The envelope carries
   `work_day_timezone` and `work_day_timezone_state`, and `day` carries
   `window_starts_at` / `window_ends_at`. While the state is
   `unconfigured` the backend counts a day as midnight-to-midnight **UTC**,
   which can put a late-afternoon visit on the next day's plan — and your
   own visit list bounds the day at the device's local midnight, so the two
   can disagree. Surface that, as `load-plan.js` does. Do **not** paper
   over it by re-bucketing client-side.
3. **Quote back what you were shown when confirming.**
   `confirm_load_plan` now takes six parameters and **requires** both
   `p_expected_revision` and `p_expected_lines_fingerprint` (`22023` if
   either is missing). The lines fingerprint is the recommendation lock: a
   Manual Keep or an owner-authorised exception changes what the plan asks
   for without moving its revision or its basis fingerprint. Take all three
   values from the plan the screen **rendered** — never re-read first, which
   would defeat the point — and on a `23514` show the sentence and re-read.
4. **Render the fourth action.** `skip` is a line the owner authorised
   going without; confirming does not transfer it. It needs its own group,
   its authorisation reason, and **no crew override control** — a keep
   against a skipped line records the durable keep for the next build and
   deliberately leaves the owner's line alone.
5. **Four states, not two.** Keep no response, a refusal (`42501`), a
   conflict (`23514` / `23505`) and accepted work distinct in the outbox
   and on screen. Do not invent success fields the receipts do not carry.
6. **Closing counts.** The current basis is the live usable balance, named
   in the receipt as `variance_basis`; a morning plan snapshot is not a
   current balance. A day closes with no plan at all. An estimated count
   stays estimated. `damaged` means units moved from usable to damaged, not
   written off, and a positive variance explained as `damaged` is refused
   with an explanation.
